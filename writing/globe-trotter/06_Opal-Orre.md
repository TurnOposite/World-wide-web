Laying in the sunlight

Grass across my gaze

I found some peace inside

As the rays shine on my face

A simpler moment’s solice

As glomming fate awaits

Lucky man’s in fortune

Before he becomes bait

Celestial tattoines

Why must they come in pairs

A single pillar’s ruins

Only shoulders this much care

Oh I m a broken man alright

Never know what s right

Still fight with all my might

figuring out how to stay bright

Rushing through opal orre

Reach the core

Last encore

My wet body ashore

Head on the floor

Can’t tell you more

Yearly. When the leafs bloom

I dream of sunflower seeds

In spring, sun escapes doom

And shines on my street

Reflecting on the loom

Blinding me with ease

Out of night, we stand confused

A first look beyond the pit
